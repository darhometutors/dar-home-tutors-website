Dar Home Tutors is a home and online tutoring organisation connecting students with dedicated tutors who provide personalised academic support.

We support learners across different education systems, including international and national curricula, with lessons tailored to their individual learning needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the questions parents commonly ask about our tutoring services.

Do you provide home tuition?

Yes. Dar Home Tutors connects students with tutors who can provide personalised tuition at home, subject to tutor availability and location.

Do you provide online tuition?

Yes. We provide live online tuition for students who prefer remote learning or require additional academic support.

Which grades do you cover?

We support students from Grades 1–12. Our services include primary, lower secondary, secondary, IGCSE and advanced-level support.

Which curricula do you support?

We support Cambridge, IGCSE, IB MYP, IBDP, NECTA and NCERT curricula.

How much does tuition cost?

Our standard hourly rates range from TSh 25,000 to TSh 50,000, depending on the student's grade. Please visit our Tuition Fees section for the current rates.

How do I request a tutor?

You can use our Request a Tutor form on the website. Submit the student's details and requirements, and your enquiry will be sent directly to us through WhatsApp.

Can I request a particular subject?

Yes. You can specify the subject you need when submitting a tutor request, and we will work to identify a suitable tutor.

How do I contact Dar Home Tutors?

You can contact us through WhatsApp or phone using the contact details provided on this website.
