About Dar Home Tutors

More than just tuition — we help students build understanding, confidence and better learning habits.

Dar Home Tutors is a Tanzania-based tutoring organisation connecting students with dedicated tutors for personalised academic support.

We provide both home and online tuition, matching students with tutors according to their academic level, subjects, curriculum and learning requirements.
